## Privacy And Capability Review

`CredentialCleanupReservation` is bounded to 1..4096 bytes and has no Debug,
Display, serialization, or material accessor. Authority hashes the material
internally and compares it with the signed cleanup descriptor. Retry compares
the exact stored material without returning it.

The implementation adds only `provisional -> ready`. It adds no cleanup read,
probe, copy, export, set, claim, or delete operation and no keyring, runtime,
config, protocol, CLI, MCP, network, or remote-effect surface. The committed
fixture contains two synthetic public Ed25519 signatures only; it contains no
private key, seed, proof, nonce, locator material, address, endpoint, mailbox
content, or provider data.
